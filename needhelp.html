Need Help?

We're here to assist you with any questions or issues you may have.

How do I place an order?

Select the item you want, click “Add to Cart”, then go to your cart and proceed to checkout.

Can I track my order?

Currently, tracking is manual. After placing an order, our team will contact you via email or phone.

I forgot my login details, what should I do?

Click on the “Forgot Password” link on the login page or contact support to reset your account.

What payment methods are accepted?

We currently accept payment on delivery and bank transfer, and online payment is also available.
